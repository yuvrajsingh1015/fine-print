Player Agreement – Starforge Online

#cats: price_change
Virtual currency has no cash value and we may change its price or the cost of items at our discretion.

#cats: fees
Purchases of virtual currency are final and cannot be refunded, even if your account is later closed.

#cats: account_termination
We can ban or suspend players for cheating, harassment or any other conduct we consider harmful to the community.

#cats: tracking
Our anti-cheat software scans running processes on your device while the game is open.

#cats: data_sharing
Aggregated gameplay statistics and your public profile may be made available to partners and esports organisers.

#cats: liability
To the extent permitted by applicable law, we will not be responsible for any loss of virtual items caused by server outages.

#cats: arbitration
Disputes will be resolved exclusively by a single arbitrator under the rules of the ICC, and you agree not to join any group action.

#cats: auto_renewal
The Battle Pass membership is billed every 30 days until you turn off renewal in your account settings.
