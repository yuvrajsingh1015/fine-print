EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

#cats: notice_period
1. Probation. The first three months of employment are a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment by giving one week's notice.

#cats: working_time
2. Hours. Your normal working hours are 37.5 hours per week. You may be required to work additional hours without further pay where the needs of the business require.

#cats: working_time
3. Place of Work. Your normal place of work is our Leeds office, but we may require you to work at any of our other sites within a reasonable travelling distance.

#cats: price_change
4. Bonus. Any bonus is entirely discretionary and may be withdrawn or varied at any time. Payment in one year does not create an entitlement in future years.

#cats: fees
5. Deductions. You agree that we may deduct from your salary any sums you owe us, including overpaid salary, unreturned equipment and the cost of training if you leave within 12 months.

#cats: side_jobs
6. Other Work. You must not, without prior written consent, engage in any other business or employment while employed by us.

#cats: side_jobs
7. Restrictions. For six months after your employment ends, you will not work for a competitor within 20 miles of our offices.

#cats: account_termination
8. Garden Leave. During any notice period we may require you not to attend work and not to contact clients or colleagues.

#cats: data_sharing
9. Data. We may share your personal data with our payroll provider, pension provider and group companies.

#cats: tracking
10. Monitoring. We monitor the use of email, internet and telephone systems to ensure compliance with company policy.

#cats: notice_period
11. Notice. After probation, you must give us one month's written notice to terminate your employment.

#
12. Holidays. You are entitled to 25 days' paid holiday per year plus public holidays.
