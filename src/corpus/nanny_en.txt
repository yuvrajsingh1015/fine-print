Part-Time Employment Contract – Retail Assistant

#cats: working_time
Your hours will vary from week to week according to the rota, which may be changed at short notice. You may be asked to work evenings, weekends and bank holidays.

#cats: price_change
The company may introduce, vary or withdraw any staff discount or benefit at its discretion.

#cats: fees
Any till shortages caused by your negligence may be recovered from your wages.

#cats: notice_period
After the first month, you are entitled to receive and required to give one week's notice for each completed year of service.

#cats: side_jobs
You must inform your manager before taking up any additional job, so that we can make sure your total hours stay within legal limits.

#cats: account_termination
We may suspend you on full pay while we investigate any allegation of misconduct.

#cats: claim_deadline
Any grievance about pay must be raised in writing within 28 days of the payment date.

#
Uniform will be provided free of charge.
