#
RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

#cats: minimum_term
1. TERM. The lease term begins on September 1 and ends on August 31 of the following year.

#cats: auto_renewal,notice_period
2. HOLDOVER. If neither party gives at least sixty (60) days' written notice before the end of the term, this lease will convert to a month-to-month tenancy at a rent 10% above the current rent.

#
3. RENT. Tenant shall pay $1,450 per month, due on the first day of each month.

#cats: fees
4. LATE CHARGE. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75 plus $10 for each additional day the rent remains unpaid.

#cats: fees
5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Landlord may deduct from the deposit unpaid rent, cleaning costs and the cost of repairing any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

#cats: fees
6. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate early only upon payment of an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

#cats: fees
7. ATTORNEY FEES. In any action to enforce this lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorney fees and costs.

#cats: liability
8. LANDLORD LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any damage or injury to Tenant, Tenant's guests or property, unless caused by Landlord's gross negligence.

#
9. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises with 24 hours' notice to make repairs.

#cats: account_termination
10. DEFAULT. If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this lease upon three days' written notice.

#cats: arbitration
11. GOVERNING LAW. This lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.
